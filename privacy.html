TransferDock Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026

TransferDock is designed to help users move selected files from external storage to Google Drive. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

Information TransferDock Uses

TransferDock may use the following information to provide the transfer workflow:

  • Files, folders, filenames, file sizes, and file metadata that you choose for a transfer.
  • Google account authorization tokens needed to upload selected files to Google Drive.
  • Transfer progress, queue status, selected destination information, and recovery state stored locally on your device.
  • Temporary upload parts staged locally while a transfer is running.

Google Drive Access

TransferDock uses Google OAuth to connect to Google Drive. It requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope, which allows the app to view and manage only the specific Drive files and folders used with TransferDock.

Google OAuth tokens are stored in the device Keychain where supported. TransferDock does not sell Google user data or use Google Drive data for advertising.

Local Storage

TransferDock stores transfer state locally so interrupted transfers can be resumed or recovered. Temporary upload parts are used only to complete transfers and may be cleared by the app when no longer needed.

Diagnostics and Support

If you choose to export support information, TransferDock may include transfer status and recovery details needed to troubleshoot the issue. Support exports are intended to avoid OAuth tokens and file contents. You should review any exported support file before sharing it.

Sharing and Tracking

TransferDock does not track users across apps or websites. TransferDock does not sell personal information. Files are uploaded only to the destination you choose, such as your Google Drive account.

Third-Party Services

Google Drive and Google OAuth are provided by Google. Your use of Google services is also governed by Google's own terms and privacy policy.
