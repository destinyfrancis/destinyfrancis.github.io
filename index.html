TransferDock

An iPad-first transfer utility for moving large media batches from external storage to Google Drive with a visible queue, resumable upload flow, and clear recovery actions.

Privacy Policy Terms of Service Support

What TransferDock Does

TransferDock helps users choose files or folders, review a transfer batch, upload selected items to Google Drive, and recover safely if a connection, account, or storage issue interrupts the workflow.

The app requests Google Drive access only for files and folders used with TransferDock, using the Google Drive drive.file scope.
